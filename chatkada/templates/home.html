About Chaya Kada

In Kerala, a "chaya kada" (tea shop) is more than just a place to grab a cup of tea—it's a community hub where people gather to chat, share news, and enjoy snacks together. Chaya Kada brings this nostalgic experience online, letting you connect with strangers and friends over virtual chai, complete with monsoon rain sounds and Malayalam music. It's your digital corner tea shop, always open! ☕🌧️

Quick Start Guide

🚀 Your First Steps

  1. Claim your daily login bonus - Visit your Profile to get 25 coins!
  2. Buy your first treat - Visit the Shop and buy some chai or snacks with your coins.
  3. Find a stranger to chat with - Click Find Chat and start a conversation!

  5. Set the mood - Click "Audio" in the nav bar to enable rain sounds and music.
  6. Explore more features - Create a private bench, open a theatre room, or complete challenges for more coins!
